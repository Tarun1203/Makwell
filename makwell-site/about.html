About

MakWell — Make it Happen

MakWell is a home electronics brand from Pushpak Enterprises, built around one idea: back every sale with real, reachable service.

MakWell makes televisions, washing machines and voltage stabilizers for Indian homes — chosen as a starting range because they cover the three things that go wrong most often in a household: the screen that won't stay sharp, the machine that can't handle daily cycles, and the power supply that damages everything else when it swings.

Every product is backed by a warranty and a service network rather than sold and forgotten. If something needs a repair, our team already knows what they're working on and where to send a technician.

Warranty

What's covered, and for how long.

Most products carry a 2-year warranty as standard, extending up to 5 years on select models, with genuine spare parts available.

Television

1 year on the full LED TV.

Washing Machine

1 year full product · 2 years spin motor · 5 years wash motor.

Stabilizer

2–5 years depending on model.

Warranty covers manufacturing defects under normal use. Physical damage, unauthorized repair and misuse are not covered.
